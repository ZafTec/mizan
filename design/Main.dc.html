02

The five rules

Numbers right, tabular

Counts and dates align on the decimal so a column scans vertically. Everything else is left.

Status is a dot plus a word

Never colour alone. The dot carries the hue, the word carries the meaning in grayscale.

One rule under the header

Full-ink. Rows are separated by a hairline. Zebra striping and vertical rules are noise.

Wash, not badge, for urgency

A dead row tints its background. It reads at a glance without a second coloured object per row.

Actions only where legal

A running job shows no buttons. Discovering the rule from a 400 is not a design.
