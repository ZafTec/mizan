Mizan · Design language · Phase 19

The secondary page

Today, History, Progress and Recipes are already a ruled sheet. The pages under More still carry the old app: icon tiles, stat cards, mascots, badge pills. This board gives them one shape so they read like the rest, and it is the shape the daily log already uses. Nothing here needs a new component; the table, the rows and the empty states exist.

Header

Title and one line that says what the page is for. No eyebrow on the pages people use, the title is enough. On a phone the primary action moves to the bottom of the page so the first screen is the content.

Facts

Up to three numbers in one ruled strip, the same strip the nutrition totals use on Today. Never a card per number, never an icon per number. If a number needs an icon to be understood, the label is wrong.

List

Rows under a section rule. Name left, numbers right in tabular figures, a chevron when the row opens something. Rows are the unit for plans, exercises, achievements, shopping items. Tables are for pages that sort and page, like Foods and Measurement history.

Action

One filled button per page, ink, and it is the thing the page exists to do. Secondary routes are links in a section header. Edit and delete live on the detail page, not on every row.

02

What leaves

× Icon tiles beside a title. The 48px coloured square carried the old brand; here it is a second coloured object per row.

× Stat cards. Four boxes counting the same list the page shows underneath.

× Mascot illustrations in empty states. An empty page says in one sentence what will appear and how to make it appear.

× Badge pills for category, muscle and equipment. They become one muted line under the name.

× Hubs that link to pages More already lists. Profile stops being a second More.

✓ Numbers always formatted. A weight is 74.6 kg, never 74.6000000000000. Deltas are text with a sign, not a coloured chip.
