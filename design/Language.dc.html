Mizan · Design language · Phase 18

Ledger

The product is a log. So the interface is a ruled sheet: hairlines instead of cards, a serif for anything a person reads and a grotesque for anything a person scans, and colour reserved for the four things that mean something. Nothing floats, nothing glows, nothing is frosted.

Verdigris survives from the old palette because it is the brand; sandy-brown and burnt-peach do not, because a fourth and fifth accent is how a status column becomes unreadable. Warn and danger are the only two hues left, and both are desaturated far enough to sit next to body text without vibrating.

04

What this replaces

Removed
×backdrop-filter on nine classes. Frosted glass over a flat background is a texture nobody asked for and a repaint every scroll.
×28–32px radii. A pill-shaped data table is a costume.
×shadow-panel on every surface. Nine floating planes means no hierarchy at all.
×Three accent ramps. Sandy-brown and burnt-peach never earned their 22 tokens.
×Inter. Correct and anonymous. This product has a thesis; the type can carry some of it.
Kept
✓Verdigris. It is the brand, and it now means exactly one thing: live.
✓The oklch ramps. Same generation method, three ramps instead of five.
✓The easing curves and durations. 120–300ms, never ease-in. Already right.
✓Dark mode as a token swap. One class, one set of variables — not a second stylesheet.
✓The compact density class. It survives; it just has less radius to shave off.
